Barcode Scanner

Point the camera at a barcode. Each scan adds a row; scanning the same code again bumps its quantity instead of duplicating it. Export the list as CSV when you're done.

Camera access is required and only granted over HTTPS. The video never leaves your device — decoding happens in the page, and there is no server here to upload it to.
