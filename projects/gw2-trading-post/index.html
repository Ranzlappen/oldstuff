GW2 Trading Post

Enter Guild Wars 2 item names or IDs, separated by commas. The lookup pulls the top three buy and sell listings for each from the official Guild Wars 2 API.

Append ;n to an item to divide its prices by n — handy when an item costs n of something to acquire, because the divided price is then directly comparable across items. Sort by a sell column to see which conversion pays best.

Item names and IDs come from a snapshot bundled with this page; only the price lookup hits the network, one request per click.
