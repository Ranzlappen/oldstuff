Rommé

Pick a seat. Open one browser tab per player — the table is shared between every tab of this browser, so a move you make appears in the others straight away.

Cards above the dashed line are your hand: other seats don't see them. Drag a card below the line to play it onto the table, where everyone does. That is exactly how the original worked — it is a virtual tabletop, not a rules engine, so melds, sequences and scoring are up to the players.
