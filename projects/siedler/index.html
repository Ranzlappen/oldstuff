Siedler Resource Table

Not the board — the bookkeeping. Five resource decks, the building stock, two dice and the robber, shared between every tab of this browser.

Draw resources into your hand (above the dashed line, where only your seat sees them). Drag a card below the line to put it on the table for a trade, or press Zur Bank to hand back everything you have on the table. Roads, settlements and cities are your own stock, so everyone can see what you have left to build.
