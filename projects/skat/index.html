Skat

Three seats. Open one browser tab per player — the table is shared between every tab of this browser.

Ten cards each plus a two-card Skat. Cards above the dashed line are your hand; drag one below it to play it into the trick, where everyone sees it. Stich einziehen sweeps the trick to your won pile. Card points follow the game (A 11, 10 10, K 4, D 3, B 2) so Augen zählen gives a real total — but bidding, trumps and who won which trick are still the players' business.
